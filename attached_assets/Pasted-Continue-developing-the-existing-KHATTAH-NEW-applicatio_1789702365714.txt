KHATTAH IS NOT A MONTHLY ROSCA.

Khattah is completion/event-driven.

A participant receives their reward when their circle is completed.

A participant can complete more than one circle in:
- one day
- one week
- one month

There must NOT be a monthly restriction on receiving rewards.

INVITATIONS

6. Completed
